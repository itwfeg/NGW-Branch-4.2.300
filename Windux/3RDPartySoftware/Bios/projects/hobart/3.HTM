AMD's Embedded Program

General Software has long been a firmware provider for AMD and its customers. Supporting AMD's full line of x86 embedded processors, General Software works closely with AMD to produce the firmware needed by OEMs to evaluate, develop for, and deploy embedded computing products.

As AMD adds products to its embedded product roadmap, General Software works closely with AMD to develop the appropriate firmware support for each reference design. For a full list of supported AMD processors, visit the General Software web site at http://www.gensw.com.

AMD provides total system solutions for the information appliance (IA) market. AMD develops reference platforms such as this one for expanding IA applications, such as interactive set-top boxes, personal access devices, residential gateways, and thin clients.

The Geode product family, featuring a wide range of silicon and system solutions, enables customers to address the needs of the information appliance market --quickly and efficiently.

AMD Corporation is enabling the expanding Information Appliance Market with the Geode product family of silicon and system solutions. Information Appliances based on Geode technology benefit from an integrated x86 processor optimized for power, performance, and price.   • Interactive Set-Top Boxes
